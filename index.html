About

From the sensor to the software, and every layer between.

I'm an embedded systems engineer with a degree in Electronics and Communications from KLE Technological University. My work sits where hardware meets software: getting a new board to boot, getting a camera to stream, and finding out why data goes missing along the way.

As a contract engineer at NeoCambrian AI, I brought up and validated multiple embedded platforms and tested high-speed camera capture from sensor to storage. Before that, I built an assistive smart-glasses prototype at EiNETCORP that combined a camera, GPS and 4G on a Raspberry Pi CM4.

I'm especially interested in robotics, computer vision, FPGA/RTL design and high-speed sensor interfaces, and in problems that need the full stack: physical interfaces and data acquisition, embedded processing, and perception. I'm looking for roles in embedded Linux, camera systems and hardware-software integration.
